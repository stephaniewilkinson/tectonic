## The short version

If tectonic plates is not what you wanted, write to **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** within
**[14 days]** of a charge and we will refund it in full. No explanation needed.

## Subscriptions

**Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Cancelling stops the
next charge; it does not refund the current period, and you keep access until that period
ends.

**The first charge.** Refundable in full within **[14 days]**, for any reason.

**Renewals.** A renewal charged while you were not using the service is refundable within
**[14 days]** of the charge. We would rather refund it than have you dispute it.

**Part-used periods.** We do not normally pro-rate a period you have used, but if something
went wrong at our end — an outage, a bug that lost your data — write to us and we will make
it right.

## Your data after a refund

**Refunding a subscription does not delete your training history.** Your account reverts to
the free tier and the data stays. If you want it gone, ask, and it is deleted.

You can export your training history at any time, on any tier, including after cancelling.
Your log should never be the thing that keeps you paying.

## Chargebacks

If you are considering one, please write to us first — the refund above is faster and we will
not argue about it.

## How to ask

Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** from the address on the account. Refunds go back to the original
payment method and normally appear within **[5–10 business days]**, depending on your bank.
