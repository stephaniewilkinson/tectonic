# Privacy Policy — moved

The published text is `views/privacy.erb`, served at `/privacy` (#682). It is the one copy to
keep true: edit it there.

Who it names and how to reach them is `PRIVACY_OPERATOR` in `app.rb`. The page stays
unpublished (404) until all three of its fields are filled in.

Two things the draft said that the app does not do were corrected on the way:

- There is no delete-my-account button, so the page says to write in to have an account deleted.
- Assistant activity logs are never pruned, so the page says they are kept as long as the
  account, not for a set period.
